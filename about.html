Our Mission

To make Christlike disciples in the nations. We are a global community of faith, committed to spreading the message of hope and love through Jesus Christ. Our mission is to reach out to all people, nurturing them in their faith journey, and empowering them to serve others.

Our Vision

To be a transforming church, impacting our community and the world. We envision a church where every member is actively engaged in ministry, where lives are transformed by the power of the Holy Spirit, and where we reflect the love of Christ in all that we do.

Our History

Founded in 1985, Nazarene Remera Church has been a beacon of faith in our community for over three decades. From humble beginnings, we have grown into a vibrant congregation, touching countless lives through our worship services, outreach programs, and discipleship ministries. Our journey is marked by God's faithfulness and the unwavering commitment of our members.
